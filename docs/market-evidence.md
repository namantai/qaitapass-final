# Market Size, Business Model & Competitor Evidence

## 1. Market Size & Kazakhstan Statistics
* **General Retail & Marketplace Context:** According to data from the Bureau of National Statistics of Kazakhstan (2025), the total e-commerce and marketplace goods turnover in 2024 reached **2,679.8 billion KZT**[cite: 4].
* **Category Share:** Computers, laptops, software, and related goods account for **5.7%** of this turnover[cite: 4].
* **Calculated Category Turnover:** $2,679.8 \text{ BN KZT} \times 5.7\% = \mathbf{152.7 \text{ billion KZT}}$[cite: 4].
* **Separation of Fact vs. Hypothesis:** The official category published by the Bureau includes brand-new retail computers, retail goods, and software. This figure **does not** represent the true Total Addressable Market (TAM) for QaitaPass, as a verified count of active B2B refurbishing companies in Kazakhstan is still unverified[cite: 4].
